The Great Work, in three minutes

Alchemists called the transformation of lead into gold the magnum opus, and Carl Jung read its four colour stages as a map of individuation: meeting the Shadow, the Anima, the dawning Self. Here each stage is a Tarot trump and a movement of 136 BPM eurodance.

  1. Nigredo · XIII Death. An eye opens on the black sun, and the Shadow looks back.
  2. Albedo · XVIII The Moon. The luminous body in the mirror; a rapper calls the Shadow and the Anima.
  3. Citrinitas · XIX The Sun. The climb up the Holy Mountain while seven chakras ignite, root to crown.
  4. Rubedo · XXI The World. The drop: the mandala of the Self, the chemical wedding, and a chorus that turns lead to gold.

After Carl Jung, the paintings of Alex Grey, the Tarot, Alejandro Jodorowsky’s The Holy Mountain, anime title cards and 1990s eurodance. Every frame is drawn by fragment shaders from the time code; the music, the vocoder and the voices were synthesized from code too.
