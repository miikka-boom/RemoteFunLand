The show

It is three in the morning and the dead have the streets. Four mismatched musicians in a dented tour van are on their way to play the Orpheum anyway.

The only voice on the radio belongs to a late-night host who keeps telling them rescue is two minutes out, and that whatever they do, they must not let the music stop.

Stay until the very end. Somebody has been taking notes.
