Two Thumbs Left

A night city, one flashlight and a horde that runs faster than you. Fight from the start room to the safe room across town, and tune the thumb sticks until they feel right.

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left: move.
  • Right thumb: aim the light. Push past the ring to fire.
  • Tap the right side to shove when they get close.

↻ Turn your phone sideways for the widest view.

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, Space to shove, R reload, Q swap, E use, H heal, C pills, G lure. Gamepads work too.
