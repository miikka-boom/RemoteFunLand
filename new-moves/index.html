No. 004 · RemoteFunLand

New Moves

Three new dances for the robots of robot_CameraWork.blend, composed from its original aerobics keys.

0:00 / 1:51

Programme

    How they were made

    Every move starts from the hand-keyed AerobicBlocking routine in the Blender file. A script samples 18 of its poses (knee lifts, arm pumps, the V, the wide squat, high knees, the tuck jump and more) and builds new ones from them.

    It can mirror a pose left to right, mix the arms of one pose with the legs of another, aim limbs to invent shapes the original never had (salutes, robot arms, jumping jacks, a hand-to-hand arm wave), turn the body or the big radio head, and keep the feet on the floor or launch a jump to a set height.

    Each dance is 64 counts: two 32-count blocks, the second mirrored or turned the other way, like a real aerobics class. The script is tools/new_moves.py.

    Sound

    Three original tracks, synthesized live with Web Audio: D minor house at 124 BPM, E dorian electro-funk at 100 BPM and an F# minor hi-NRG breakbeat at 160 BPM. No samples and no recorded music.

    Same stage as Antenna Aerobics. On slower devices the stage lowers its own resolution. With reduced motion set, the camera moves slowly and the flashes are off.
