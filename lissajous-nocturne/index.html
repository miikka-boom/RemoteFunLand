How the picture is made

Left is X, right is Y

The left channel’s waveform sets the beam’s horizontal position, sample by sample, and the right channel sets the vertical. The trace is read straight from the audio graph; nothing is drawn by hand.

Small ratios close the shape

When the two frequencies sit at a whole-number ratio such as 2 : 3, the beam returns to where it started and the figure closes. The 0.2 Hz offset makes each figure roll.

The drums skip the beam

Kick, snare, hats and bass reach your speakers through a separate bus. You see them only as the duck on each kick and in the FFT strip under the screen.
