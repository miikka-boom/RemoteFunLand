The Alchemist’s Spread

Three cards from the Tarot de Marseille, drawn at random, open into three painted tableaux in the manner of Jodorowsky’s The Holy Mountain: the past in black, the present in white, the future in red, then the climb to gold.

    About the piece

    After Alejandro Jodorowsky’s The Holy Mountain (1973) and the Tarot de Marseille, which he later helped restore. The three cards follow the alchemical order of nigredo, albedo and rubedo; the ascent ends in gold, and then the camera pulls back.

    Every image and sound is made in your browser while you watch: a 2D canvas at 30 frames a second with no images, video or samples, which lowers its own resolution if your device falls behind. Each draw is one of 9,240 and can be shared by its link.
