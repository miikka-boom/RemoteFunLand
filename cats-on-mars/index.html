A lowtech demo and music video. A transmission from Mars, a cat in a space suit, a song about the nature of life. Born, grow, play, sleep.

Nothing here is a recording. The song is synthesized in your browser when the page opens (the “precalc”): drums, bass, chip arpeggios, FM bells, Martian wind, purring, and a formant-synthesis singer that sings the lyrics from phonemes and meows on cue. Every frame is drawn pixel by pixel into a 320×180 buffer by plain JavaScript and dithered to the Amiga’s 4096 colours: rotating Mars, voxel landscapes, copper bars, plasma, a tunnel, a rotozoomer, vector balls, metaballs, the Game of Life and a DNA helix.
