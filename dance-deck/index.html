How to play

Drag a move from the deck onto any of the eight slots. Each slot is two counts, so the loop is a 16-count routine that plays forever. Drag a slot onto another to swap them, or off the loop to remove it. You can also tap a move, then tap a slot.

Sound on starts the beat. Tempo and beat change on the next bar. Mirror flips the left half of the crew, Canon makes each dancer follow one count behind, and Copy link saves the routine in the address.

Where the moves come from

Every card is sampled from clips exported from robot_CameraWork.blend: its hand-keyed aerobics, the New Moves dances built from them, the story poses from Human Mode, and Human mode, the file’s raw motion capture, which ignores the grid on purpose. The card pictures are rendered from the robot when the page loads.

Sound

Four synthesized loops, house, hi-NRG, breakbeat and funk, made with Web Audio. No samples. Works on phones; on slower devices the stage lowers its own resolution, and with reduced motion set the camera stops circling.
