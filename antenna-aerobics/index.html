No. 003 · RemoteFunLand

Antenna Aerobics

The robots from robot_CameraWork.blend take a 90-second class, then throw a party.

0:00 / 1:30

Programme

    The dancers

    Every robot is the one from robot_CameraWork.blend: its 334-vertex mesh, its 20-joint rig and the hand-keyed AerobicBlocking routine, exported from Blender and baked for the browser. The routine is 32 counts long, the standard aerobics block, so each pass starts on a downbeat.

    The file keys most poses with stepped interpolation, so the limbs snap from pose to pose. That is the default here. Motion: smooth switches to a version where the same keys are joined by curves.

    In Human mode the soloist plays the raw motion capture miikka_dance_long from the same file, retargeted onto the robot and slowed to the half-time groove. The trio walks off with the file’s KristaWalk cycle, and the hidden “PARTY” letters and sphere become the sign and the disco ball.

    Sound

    The score is original and synthesized live with Web Audio at 150 BPM in A minor: drum machine, octave bass, house stabs, an arpeggiator and a lead hook. No samples and no recorded music. The track the Blender file was timed to is not part of this repo.

    Built for laptops and phones. On slower devices the stage lowers its own resolution. With reduced motion set, the camera cuts less and the flashes are off.
