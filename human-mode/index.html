Human Mode

A music video. Sixteen robots rehearse in perfect sync until Unit 04 glitches into human mode.

0:00 / 3:12

Running order

    The story

    Sixteen units rehearse the routine from robot_CameraWork.blend in a cold, white hall, every knee lift on the one and every arm on the two. During “hold the pose”, Unit 04’s dome starts to flicker.

    On the chorus it goes human: it dances the file’s raw motion capture, loose and off the grid, while the others stop and stare. The red alarm goes off, everyone points, someone tries to reboot it, and then the glitch spreads.

    After one beat of dead silence, all sixteen are human under the PARTY sign, with a Soul Train line, a quiet moment for Unit 04 on the pedestal, and a final freeze that every robot hits a little late.

    How it’s made

    The video runs live in the browser, synced to the song’s measured beat grid (122 BPM). The lights follow the track’s own kick and hi-hats. The robots, their stepped routine, the New Moves dances and the motion capture all come from the Blender file, exported with tools/export_robots.py and tools/human_mode_moves.py.

    The song is the WAV in this folder, played as a 160 kbps MP3. With reduced motion set, the camera holds one wide shot and the flashes are off. Click the progress bar to jump.
