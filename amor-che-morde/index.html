Synopsis

Act One. A village square at sunset. Livia marries Amadeo. The Contessa Velena, who had him first, proposes a toast. The wine is poisoned. Livia dies, at length.

Act Two. The cemetery at midnight. Ossobuco the gravedigger complains that nobody stays buried any more. Amadeo weeps at Livia’s grave and begs her to come back. She does. She still loves him. She is also very hungry.

Act Three. The same, a little later. The dead rise in chorus. The villagers arrive with torches, and the Contessa with them, briefly. Amadeo makes Livia an offer she cannot refuse.
